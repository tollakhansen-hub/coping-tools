Not sure what is happening?

The feeling does not need a perfect name.

Pick the thing that is easiest to notice right now.

My body feels unsettled
Tense, shaky, restless, tight or physically wound up.My thoughts keep going
Replaying, predicting, worrying or trying to solve something over and over.Everything feels like too much
Too many things need attention and you cannot work out where to start.I feel flat or stuck
Low energy, numb, heavy or finding it hard to begin anything.Something has just happened

Choose whichever is bothering you most right now.
