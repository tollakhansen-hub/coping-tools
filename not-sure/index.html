None of these quite fit?

Sometimes it is difficult to put what you are experiencing into words. You might recognise parts of several options, or none of them.

You do not need to find an exact match. The five guides are starting points, not fixed categories. You can explore whichever feels closest, even if only part of it fits.

If none feels right, there is no need to force a choice. You can pause and come back whenever you want.

Why we say this: Research on identifying emotions and overlapping emotional difficulties helps explain why a single description may not fit everyone’s experience. These studies did not test this page.
