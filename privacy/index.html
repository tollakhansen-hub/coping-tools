Privacy

coping.tools is designed to collect as little personal information as possible.

This static site does not include accounts, contact forms, advertising or behavioural profiling.

The live site currently includes Cloudflare's Web Analytics/RUM performance beacon, injected by the hosting layer. Cloudflare states that the beacon does not use cookies or browser storage and does not collect or use visitors' personal data; it collects page-view and performance information. Cloudflare's hosting layer may also process basic request data while serving and protecting the site.

If privacy-affecting features are added later, this page must be updated before or when those features go live.

Links to research and guidance may take you to external websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices.
