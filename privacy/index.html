Privacy

coping.tools is designed to collect as little personal information as possible.

This static site does not include accounts, contact forms, advertising or behavioural profiling.

Cloudflare Web Analytics/RUM is enabled on public pages to measure page views and page performance. Its browser beacon sends those measurements to Cloudflare and may include a page-load ID and referring page. Cloudflare says the beacon does not use or access cookies or browser storage, and does not track end users across its customers’ sites. Cloudflare receives your IP address as part of normal request handling and says it discards it at the nearest Cloudflare data centre rather than storing it in the core RUM databases or logs. Cloudflare’s standard HTTP request processing and logs remain separate from RUM. The install counter, described below, stores only event names and aggregate totals.

To understand whether the optional app-install feature is useful, coping.tools keeps anonymous aggregate counts of install-button presses, accepted browser prompts, browser-confirmed installs, and first opens of the installed app on iPhone and iPad. On iPhone and iPad, the app stores a flag on your device after reporting its first Home Screen open. The flag helps avoid repeat counts and stays on your device. These counters store event names and totals. They do not store IP addresses, device or browser identifiers, account data, or which pages a visitor viewed. Only the site maintainer can view the totals.

Each coping page also has an optional “Was this page useful?” question. When you choose Yes or No, only an aggregate count for that page, answer and UTC date is stored. No names, diagnoses, personal comments, IP addresses, device identifiers or individual voting histories are kept in this feedback database. Only the site maintainer can access the aggregate feedback report. The answers are not a measure of clinical effectiveness.

When you press Print or Save PDF on a coping route, coping.tools records an anonymous aggregate count for the route, action and UTC date. A print request does not confirm that a page was printed, and a PDF save means a download was started. The guide content is prepared on your device; it is not uploaded to the counter. The counter does not store names, IP addresses, diagnoses, or individual action histories. These totals can be seen only by the site maintainer.

The maintainer’s private statistics page uses a secure, HttpOnly sign-in cookie that expires after 30 days or when the maintainer signs out. The statistics token is used only to sign in and is not stored in the page or URL. The private statistics page is excluded from the site’s offline cache. Opening the private page does not submit any votes or print events.

To reduce accidental repeat votes, your browser keeps a small page-specific “answered” flag in local storage when available, with session storage as a fallback. It stays on your device and is not transmitted to coping.tools; it contains neither your vote nor an identifier. Each coping page can be answered independently. The flag normally remains after closing and reopening the browser or app, but may not persist in private browsing or when browser storage is restricted. You can remove it by clearing this site's data. This is a browser-level safeguard rather than a guarantee against duplicate votes.

coping.tools attempts to save copies of its public pages, fonts and other required files in your browser for offline use, including all five coping routes. Some pages may be unavailable offline if they could not be saved successfully. These copies stay on your device; API responses are excluded from this offline cache. Offline copies may be older than the live site. You can remove them by clearing this site's browser data.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the applicable data-protection law and circumstances, you may have rights to request access to personal data, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, or a copy of data, and to complain to the relevant data-protection supervisory authority. You can contact contact@coping.tools about a privacy question or request. We may need information to verify and handle a request, but please do not send sensitive health information unnecessarily.

Because the site's own feedback and usage counters are aggregated without individual visitor records, it may not be possible to identify or retrieve a particular person's contribution to those totals. Cloudflare's processing of requests to deliver and protect the site is separate from these counters.

External services

If you choose to support coping.tools using the Support this project link, you leave this site and interact with PayPal. PayPal handles any account, payment and transaction information you provide under its own privacy statement. PayPal may share transaction information with the recipient as needed to complete and record a payment. coping.tools does not operate the payment page or control what information PayPal collects. Read PayPal’s privacy statement.

Why information is processed

Cloudflare processes network requests to deliver the website, maintain its availability and help protect it from abuse. The site's aggregate counters help the maintainer understand basic feature use and improve the site without building visitor profiles. If you email the maintainer, the information in your message is used to read and respond to your enquiry. The legal basis for each processing activity and relevant retention arrangements are under review; this notice should not be read as confirmation that a complete GDPR compliance assessment has been completed.

Cloudflare and email providers may process information in other countries under their own applicable data-processing arrangements. The precise transfer safeguards and retention periods for these services have not yet been fully verified.

If privacy-affecting features are added later, this page must be updated before or when those features go live.

Links to research and guidance may take you to external websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices.
