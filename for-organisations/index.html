For organisations

Practical support, freely available.

coping.tools offers short, clearly written exercises for difficult moments — when someone feels overwhelmed, restless, caught in repetitive thoughts, low in energy or affected by a difficult event.

Healthcare services, schools, libraries, community organisations and others are welcome to explore the tools and consider sharing them with the people they support.

Free to use · No sign-up · Easy to share

coping.tools maintains the wording and research notes. Each organisation decides whether a resource is appropriate for the people it supports.

How the tools are developed

Each tool begins with a manageable step for the next minute, followed by options for a little later. The approach draws on established psychological principles, relevant research and practical experience. Evidence notes and review information are available on the individual pages.

The tools offer practical support alongside, rather than in place of, professional care.

Support the project

coping.tools is independently maintained and free to use. Organisations that value the resource are welcome to enquire about contributing toward its maintenance, accessibility and ongoing evidence reviews.

Support is entirely voluntary and does not influence how tools are selected or reviewed.
