Coping Toolbox

Other ways people cope

Our five practical guides are the main focus of coping.tools. This optional collection offers additional everyday activities and printable lists to explore at your own pace, alone or together with someone else.

Activities are choices, not instructions or clinical treatment plans. What feels helpful differs from person to person.

Private experimental version · No forms, accounts, or activity tracking on this page.

When everything feels intense

Choose what feels suitable. You do not need to try everything.

  • □ Sit somewhere quieter
  • □ Take a gentle walk
  • □ Listen to familiar music
  • □ Hold a comfortable object
  • □ Ask someone to sit nearby
  • □ Try a simple repetitive activity
