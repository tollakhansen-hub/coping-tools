# Evidence wording review — 8 November 2026

Status: implemented on a review branch; not approved for production.
Scope: evidence summaries in all five routes and the general research overview of Practical support. Exercises, safety instructions and the five printable care sections are unchanged.

## Method and limits
Targeted verification of publisher full text for Gordon, Wei, Jia and Dong; indexed original abstracts for Cuijpers, Donato and Devilly; official WHO and IFRC pages. No trial-level reanalysis or exhaustive systematic review. Targeted correction/retraction searches found no notices for the main new reviews; absence in search is not a guarantee. Prior evidence records and contrary findings remain retained.

## Sources and decisions
- Gordon et al. 2026, DOI 10.1371/journal.pdig.0001764: 147 RCTs, 26,391 participants. Immediate g: wellbeing .36, depression .44, anxiety .40, stress .46, quality of life .30, flexibility .41. Substantial heterogeneity, smaller effects against active controls; 120 trials some bias concerns, 24 high risk, 3 low risk. Moderate GRADE certainty for most immediate outcomes, low for quality of life. Add as programme-level background to Mind, not a test of a prompt or proof of a mechanism.
- Wei et al. 2025, DOI 10.1186/s40359-025-03348-x: 29 RCTs, 2,535 participants, rumination SMD -.51 (95% CI -.64 to -.39). Follow-up: 14 trials, 1,358 participants, SMD -.61 (-.89 to -.32), I² 82%; sensitivity estimate -.41 (-.59 to -.22). No clear follow-up advantage over CBT or psychoeducation. Retain structured-programme distinction.
- Dong et al. 2026, DOI 10.2147/JMDH.S610845: 42 studies, 8,798 participants, mixed designs and narrative synthesis. Supports immediate participation and decision processes in severe mental illness. Not 42 RCTs; application to everyday support is indirect. Add only to general care research overview.
- Jia et al. 2025, DOI 10.2196/68054: 17 RCTs overall, 12 in meta-analysis; depression benefits at 2/3/6 months, quality of life at 3/6 months. Twelve-month depression null: two studies, n=548, I² 89%. Do not describe this as loss of quality-of-life benefit at twelve months or proof all benefits fade. Some interventions multicomponent. Add qualified low-route summary.
- Cuijpers et al. 2026, DOI 10.1016/j.cpr.2026.102783: 105 trials, 13,933 participants overall; adult outpatient control comparison k=61, SMD .67 (.54 to .80), I² 71%, prediction interval -.11 to 1.46. Self-guided k=15, SMD .36 (.20 to .51). Broad review reports benefit to 12 months after randomization. Do not equate this time point/population with Jia's narrower digital analysis or claim equivalence from nonsignificance versus other therapies.
- Donato et al. 2026, DOI 10.1016/j.jpsychores.2026.112563: 31 RCTs, 2,277 participants overall, selected for sleep outcomes. Anxiety SMD -1.11 (-1.69 to -.53), I² 85%. Do not imply all 31 measured anxiety; exact anxiety subset not established here. Retain full-programme scope; remove duplicate disclaimers.
- Devilly et al. 2026, DOI 10.1016/j.brat.2026.105124: 84 randomized, 74 followed up after stress video; greater immediate reduction in controls, no overall two-week difference, concerning initial-distress association in PFA arm. Retain this finding with laboratory/group context, not blanket harm claims.
- WHO self-help implementation guide (1 June 2026): https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240120785 . Guided/unguided delivery; highlighted trials used trained supervised helper support over five weeks. Context only, not validation or endorsement of coping.tools.
- IFRC Guidelines 2025 (official download released March 2026): https://www.ifrc.org/document/ifrc-international-first-aid-resuscitation-and-education-guidelines-2025 . Professional guidance for practical, non-intrusive support, not PTSD-prevention efficacy evidence.

## Editorial decisions
Lead with supported benefits; state the adaptation boundary once in each route's visible summary or immediate expansion. Preserve worry-postponement null/adverse-direction findings, RESPOND missing-data and follow-up limitations, PFA concerns, breathing active-control null, and uncertainty about overwhelm.
No new exercise, diagnosis, physiological reset, guaranteed relief, or site efficacy claim.
Practical support's section-specific text is unchanged; the five section PDF links and their source section text remain unchanged. PDFs are not regenerated for this webpage-only research overview update.
Sitemap and update-history changes are staged with the content for eventual publication.
