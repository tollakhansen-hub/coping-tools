# I feel low or shut down — behavioural activation evidence appraisal

Reviewed: 7 November 2026. Scope: internal research record; no change to the coping exercises or public page.

## Primary evidence: Cuijpers et al. (2026)

Cuijpers P, Ciharova M, Tong L, Liu Y, Sprenger AA, Miguel C, Karyotaki E, Harrer M. *Behavioral activation for depression: A comprehensive systematic review and meta-analysis.* Clinical Psychology Review. 2026;128:102783. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpr.2026.102783 ; PMID: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42492146/ .

- Systematic review and meta-analysis of **105 randomised trials, 13,933 participants**; **40 trials** classified as low risk of bias.
- Adult outpatient behavioural activation (BA) vs controls: **SMD 0.67**, 95% CI **0.54–0.80**, **61 trials**; substantial heterogeneity (**I² 71%**), and 95% prediction interval **−0.11 to 1.46** (the effect may vary markedly in future settings).
- **Self-guided BA**: **15 trials**, **SMD 0.36**, 95% CI **0.20–0.51**. This is especially relevant to the site's unguided access, but these were BA interventions rather than isolated minute-long coping prompts.
- BA compared with other psychotherapies: **27 trials**, SMD **0.04**, 95% CI **−0.10 to 0.18**; no significant difference.
- Adult BA effects remained significant at 12 months in the authors' analysis; this does not establish sustained effects for the site's individual exercises.
- Too few trials for reliable estimates in children/adolescents or BA vs medication.

### Interpretation for coping.tools

This is the **primary supporting source** for the route's action-before-motivation principle and the plausibility of self-guided action-oriented support. It is evidence for structured behavioural activation for depression, **not** evidence that opening curtains, drinking water, stepping outside briefly, or preparing an item for later independently reduces depressive symptoms or relieves shutdown. Do not label the site as providing depression treatment or a validated BA protocol.

The route's permission to rest or reduce stimulation, lack of pressure to experience an immediate mood change, and referral to professional help when difficulties persist remain important. Preserve these and the optional, non-diagnostic framing.

### Evidence balance and caveats

- BA effects vary considerably; the adult outpatient analysis has high heterogeneity and a prediction interval spanning zero.
- Most included trials were **not** rated low risk of bias (40/105 low risk).
- Self-guided interventions have smaller average effects than the broader adult outpatient comparison; these subgroup estimates are not a head-to-head randomised comparison of delivery modes.
- The short action-planning component study already cited on the page reported only very small symptom reductions; keep this contextual limitation.
- Do not infer that choosing a particular activity is superior without direct comparative evidence.

## Editorial decision

Make Cuijpers et al. (2026) the **lead internal research reference**. The public evidence section already links to the review and accurately states the distinction between treatment programmes and the page's short steps. **No public copy, dates, PDF, or exercises need changing** for this evidence appraisal. Retain other evidence, including mixed/null findings, rather than replacing it.
