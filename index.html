How coping.tools works

coping.tools organizes coping tools around what is happening in the moment rather than around a diagnosis. Each route starts with something small enough to try in the next minute, then offers options for a few minutes or later in the day. The collection focuses on short-term coping, with evidence and limits shown on each route. If more than one situation fits, choose whichever feels most noticeable right now. It does not diagnose conditions or replace medical or psychological care.
